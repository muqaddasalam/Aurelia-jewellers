How we began

Made slowly,
on purpose.

Aurelia started in a small workshop with the simple idea that jewellery should feel personal — not mass-produced. We work with independent artisans who still finish every clasp and stone setting by hand.

We source recycled gold and conflict-free stones whenever possible. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is made just because it’s trending.

The result is pieces that age with you, not against you.
